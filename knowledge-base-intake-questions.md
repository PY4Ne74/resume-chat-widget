# Knowledge base intake questions

Answer any/all of these (skip what doesn't apply) and send back to Claude — each answer becomes a
new grounded case-study entry in `knowledge-base.json` that the chat widget can cite. No need to
polish the writing; rough notes are fine, they'll get reshaped into the case-study format.

## Industries/situations not on the resume
1. Have you worked with or advised businesses outside SaaS/cybersecurity/education/automotive —
   e.g. professional services, healthcare, real estate, e-commerce brands, nonprofits? Even
   informally (freelance, advising a friend's business, a side project)?
2. What's the smallest business/budget you've ever gotten real marketing results for — a solo
   founder or pre-revenue startup scenario?
3. Have you ever worked with a B2C brand, or is everything on your resume B2B?

## The "how," not just the "what"
4. When you built the AI-accelerated production pipeline at IntelliThreat (Claude/Higgsfield/
   Remotion), what's the actual workflow — what used to take a week that now takes a day,
   concretely?
5. Walk me through *how* you got the 20% lead-to-customer conversion rate at IntelliThreat —
   what specifically made it convert so much better than typical benchmarks (targeting?
   messaging? sales handoff?)?
6. For the 6.4x ROAS at Accutrain — what was the actual insight or tactic that drove that,
   versus just "ran ads well"?
7. How did you actually turn around the lowest-performing sales team at Priority Chevrolet in a
   year — what did you change first?

## Objection handling
8. Are you looking for full-time employment, consulting/fractional work, project-based
   engagements, or open to all three?
9. Are you open to remote-only, or willing to relocate/travel? Any geographic constraints?
10. What's your ideal company stage/size to join (early startup vs. scale-up vs. established
    company)?
11. What's a realistic timeline before a company you join sees measurable results — 30 days,
    90 days, 6 months?
12. Is there a type of role, company, or industry you'd turn down even if the money was right?

## Depth on tools/technical range
13. Beyond Claude/Higgsfield/Remotion, what's your actual comfort level with things like
    marketing automation platforms (HubSpot workflows, email sequencing logic), analytics/
    attribution modeling, or CRM administration — hands-on builder or strategist-who-directs-
    others?
14. Have you built or overseen anything with paid social beyond Facebook/LinkedIn — TikTok,
    YouTube, programmatic display, ABM platforms?
15. What's your actual SEO experience — technical SEO, content-led SEO, both, or mostly
    paid-channel focused?

## Leadership/working style
16. What's your management philosophy when leading a team of specialists (like the 18 direct/
    extended at IntelliThreat or 20+ at TechArk) — how hands-on vs. delegating are you?
17. Describe a time a campaign or strategy failed or underperformed — what happened and what
    did you change?
18. How do you typically work with a CEO/founder day-to-day — are you the person building the
    plan and asking for sign-off, or executing someone else's vision?

## Proof/credibility extras
19. Do you have a case study, client, or manager who'd give a strong reference or testimonial
    quote we could paraphrase (with permission) — even a short one?
20. Is there a specific number or outcome you're proudest of that *isn't* on the resume because
    it's harder to quantify (e.g., a relationship saved, a team member you developed, a
    strategic pivot that avoided disaster)?
